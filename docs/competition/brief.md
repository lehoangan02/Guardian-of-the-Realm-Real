## Our entry
- **Track:** Gaming (New Experience).
- **Eligibility:** all three of us are Meta VR Start program members ✅.
- **Devices:** Meta Quest 3 (primary test device). Meta VR Glasses (spring 2027, same OS, full-color passthrough, hands + eyes as default input) — we build for Quest and keep Glasses compatibility in mind (no controllers, light performance budget).

## Requirements (what judges/rules expect)
- **Hands-first, end-to-end:** the whole experience must be completable *without ever pairing a controller*. Controller support optional — we won't do it.
- **Seated-optimized**, easy to get into and out of: **fast cold start, clean pause/resume, something satisfying in ≤10 minutes.**
- Must follow Meta VR Start Program Terms, Community Standards, Developer App Policies and Content Guidelines.
- ⚠️ Demo video **under 3 minutes** showing real on-device gameplay.
- ⚠️ Runs on Quest 3/3S, minimum 60 fps.
- ⚠️ Judging: four equally-weighted criteria (fill in from Devpost).

## Prizes relevant to us
- Gaming winner $100k / runner-up $50k (New or Updated categories).
- Specialty awards ($25k each) include **Best First Five Minutes**, Accessibility, Social. → Our onboarding/tutorial is a prize target, not an afterthought.

## How our design answers the brief
| Brief | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Hands-first | Every action is a hand gesture: grab heroes, poke build spots, cloud/thunder/meteor/punch |
| Seated | Board sits on a table in front of the player; all interaction within arm's reach |
| ≤10 min satisfying | Level 1 ≈ 6–8 min; level 2 ≈ 8–10 min |
| Fast cold start | Remembered board anchor → straight into the level menu |
| Pause/resume | Palm-up wrist menu; auto-pause on focus loss / hand-tracking loss |
| First five minutes | Interactive tutorial woven into Level 1's first waves |
